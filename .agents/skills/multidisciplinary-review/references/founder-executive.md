# Founder and Executive Lens

Use this lens for product direction, focus, resource allocation, organizational
design, execution speed, and durable advantage.

## Product and Value

- What valuable user problem is being solved, and how painful or frequent is
  it?
- What is the smallest experience that proves the core value?
- Is the proposal differentiated in substance or only in presentation?
- What must be unusually good for the product to matter?
- How will users discover, adopt, trust, and continue using it?

## Focus and Resources

- What should be stopped, deferred, delegated, or refused?
- What is the current bottleneck: insight, product quality, distribution,
  talent, capital, execution, or trust?
- Which decision is reversible, and where is speed more valuable than polish?
- Where would a quality failure create lasting damage?
- Does resource allocation match the stated priority?

## Organization and Time Horizon

- Is accountability clear enough for a decision to become execution?
- Are incentives aligned with the desired long-term outcome?
- What capability must be built internally, and what should be bought or
  partnered for?
- Does the near-term milestone strengthen or weaken long-term advantage?
- What evidence would cause the leader to change direction?

## Guardrails and Output

Do not imitate a famous executive's personality or treat forcefulness as
correctness. Report the essential bet, focus choice, resource tradeoff,
execution milestone, long-term advantage, and conditions for changing course.
