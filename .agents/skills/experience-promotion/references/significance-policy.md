# Significance Policy

Use this reference to decide whether an event deserves durable treatment.

## Evidence Record

Keep these distinct:

- **Observation:** what directly happened;
- **Candidate explanation:** a plausible causal account;
- **Alternatives:** other accounts compatible with the evidence;
- **Confidence:** low, medium, or high, with a reason when material;
- **Human signal:** explicit feedback about importance or desired behavior.

Human feedback is strong evidence about operational value, but it does not automatically verify causation or generality.

## Promotion Criteria

Assess only as deeply as needed:

- **Significance:** would recurrence materially affect correctness, safety, cost, recovery, or user trust?
- **Generality:** does it recur across tasks or projects, or only in one local setting?
- **Stability:** is the lesson likely to survive model, tool, and environment changes?
- **Novelty:** is it absent from the existing canonical guidance or mechanism?
- **Evidence:** is it supported beyond coincidence or a single ambiguous event?
- **Compression:** can the decision value be preserved in a shorter or more general form?
- **Correct scope:** what is the narrowest audience that needs it?
- **Mitigation value:** would an intervention actually change future outcomes?
- **Reversibility:** how easily can the intervention be corrected or removed?

Frequency is evidence of recurrence, not a substitute for significance. A rare severe privacy near-miss may matter more than a frequent harmless warning.

## Outcomes

- **Discard:** no durable decision value.
- **Keep transient:** relevant only to the current task.
- **Capture episode:** important evidence, but not yet generalizable.
- **Candidate lesson:** plausible reuse with adequate evidence.
- **Candidate mechanism or policy:** sufficiently stable and consequential to justify validation at broader scope.

Do not turn a single failure into an absolute rule. Prefer a scoped hypothesis and identify what future observation would strengthen or falsify it.
