# Prototype Ladder

## Rungs

| Rung | Purpose | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `D0` | concept, paper, or rule simulation | `E0` to `E1` |
| `P0` | isolate a mechanic | `E2` |
| `P1` | test feel or readability | `E2` to `E3` |
| `P2` | test interacting systems | `E2` to `E4` |
| `VS` | representative quality across disciplines | `E2` to `E5` |
| `A0` | complete alpha core | `E2` to `E6` |
| `RC` | release-candidate validation | evidence depends on claim |

Choose the lowest rung that can falsify the current hypothesis. Do not call all
early work `v0`.

## Experiment Brief

Record question, hypothesis, tested change, controlled variables, prototype
version, participant or automation type, observations, success signal, failure
signal, kill criterion, effort bound, decision branches, and next step.

For subjective alternatives prefer pairwise questions: which is more readable,
more interesting, repetitive sooner, more expressive of identity, or more likely
to invite another voluntary play?

## Vertical Slice Rule

Use a vertical slice when cross-discipline coexistence is the uncertainty. Make
one representative short segment strong enough to evaluate feel, systems, art,
audio, pacing, and UX together rather than spreading partial quality across the
whole game.

## Promotion Boundary

Implementation completion is not experiment success. Decide using the observed
signal and predeclared branch. Preserve failed results when they prevent repeated
work.
