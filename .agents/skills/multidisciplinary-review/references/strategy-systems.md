# Strategy and Systems Lens

Use this lens for business choices, operating models, architecture, incentives,
cross-functional dependencies, and implementation design.

## Strategic Diagnosis

- What decision must be made, by whom, and by when?
- What outcome matters, for which stakeholder, and how will it be measured?
- Is the stated problem a cause, a symptom, a constraint, or an aspiration?
- What is the comparison set and the credible alternative, including doing
  nothing?
- Where is advantage created, captured, defended, or lost?
- Which uncertainty most affects the decision?

## System Design

- Define the system boundary, actors, interfaces, flows, state, feedback loops,
  and external dependencies.
- Identify bottlenecks, single points of failure, coupling, delayed effects,
  incentives, and local optimizations that damage the whole.
- Separate reversible from irreversible choices and preserve options where
  uncertainty is high.
- Evaluate technical feasibility, operating burden, governance, migration,
  failure recovery, and total cost rather than initial build cost alone.

## Action Design

- Convert recommendations into an owner, next action, decision date, leading
  indicator, outcome metric, and stop or escalation condition.
- Sequence work by dependency and information gain.
- Prefer a small test when it can resolve the main uncertainty more cheaply
  than a large plan.

## Output

Provide the issue structure, system-level diagnosis, options and tradeoffs,
recommended path, implementation sequence, metrics, and principal risk.
