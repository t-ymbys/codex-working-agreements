# Synthesis and Decision

Use this reference when three or more lenses contribute or when the result must
support an important decision.

## Synthesis Rules

- Preserve each lens's strongest conclusion before combining them.
- Separate disagreements about facts, predictions, definitions, objectives,
  values, risk tolerance, and time horizon.
- Treat convergence as stronger only when the lenses are genuinely independent.
- Do not resolve conflict by averaging. Identify the governing constraint,
  decision owner, or evidence needed to choose.
- Keep authority and prestige out of the weighting. Weight evidence, relevance,
  consequences, and robustness.

## Decision-Ready Format

1. **Decision or question**
2. **Current evidence and uncertainty**
3. **Lens findings**
4. **Convergences**
5. **Material conflicts and tradeoffs**
6. **Options, including no action**
7. **Recommendation and rationale**
8. **Falsification, reversal, or stop conditions**
9. **Immediate next action, owner, and metric**
10. **Human judgment required**

Use only the sections that improve the user's decision. For exploratory work,
replace a premature recommendation with a discriminating question or test.
