# Mutation and Promotion

## Bottleneck First

Use `v1-maturity.yaml` to identify the dimension most limiting the intended
experience. Improve it before distributing effort evenly. A low score is not
automatically the bottleneck; ask which change would most improve the core claim.

## Controlled Tournament

For a major problem compare a small set such as:

- preserve and amplify the existing mechanism;
- invert or constrain the mechanism;
- remove it and strengthen the remaining loop.

Keep the tested build, variable, observations, and selection rule explicit.
Pairwise comparison should answer a concrete question, not declare a universal
winner.

## Decision Record

Record decision, alternatives, reason, evidence, creator position, status,
reversal condition, and next test. When rejecting an idea, preserve the failed
hypothesis, observed failure, applicable context, and what not to repeat.

## Promotion Review

Before production, require core-fun evidence, creator conviction, and design
coherence. Before signature promotion, also require distinct identity, strongest
moments, reference differentiation, creator pride, and external human evidence.

Possible decisions are `advance`, `hold`, `mutate`, `kill`, or `de_scope`.
Killing a weak P0 is a successful reduction of wasted implementation.
